couple things to think about. 
1. Sparse signal (temporal)
The CBF constraint L_g_h @ u >= -alpha * h — alpha only matters when the robot is close to the obstacle AND approaching it. That's maybe 5-10 steps out of a 50-80 step episode (~10-15% of timesteps). The other 85-90% of timesteps, any alpha produces identical outcomes. PPO averages gradients over all timesteps, so the meaningful signal is drowned.

2. Risk asymmetry
When alpha does matter (near obstacle):

High alpha → direct path → +small progress reward, but risk of collision (-100)
Low alpha → CBF pushes wider → -small progress cost, but guaranteed safe
The -100 collision penalty is catastrophic compared to the marginal progress benefit. PPO becomes risk-averse and favors low alpha.
