Experiment 61: A* Navigation + Moving Obstacles
=================================================
NOT YET RUN

Motivation:
-----------
All previous experiments used static obstacles. Real-world obstacles
move (pedestrians, other robots, etc). Moving obstacles test whether
alpha/phi can handle a dynamic environment where the CBF must
continuously adapt.

Key changes from Exp 57:
------------------------
- Obstacles have constant velocity: U(0.3, 1.0) m/s, random direction
- Velocity sampled once per episode, constant throughout
- Obstacles bounce off y-boundaries to stay in play
- A* replans every 10 steps (was 50) to track moving obstacles

Setup:
------
- Action: [alpha, phi] — kx, ky from A*
- SHIELD SDF CBF, collision -250
- 3 moving obstacles
- Everything else same as exp 57

What we hope to find:
---------------------
1. Alpha/phi respond not just to proximity but to obstacle dynamics
2. The CBF activates more frequently since obstacles can move into
   the robot's path unexpectedly
3. Stronger training signal for phi since static avoidance isn't enough

Note: FPS may be lower due to frequent A* replanning (every 10 steps).
