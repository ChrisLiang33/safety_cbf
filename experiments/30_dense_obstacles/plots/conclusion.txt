Experiment 30: 6 Dense Obstacles + Radius Noise + Constant Bias
=================================================================

Question:
With only 3 obstacles, the robot often has wide open paths. With 6 obstacles,
the environment is much denser — tighter gaps, more constrained navigation.
Does the higher obstacle density force phi to show stronger adaptive behavior?

Hypothesis:
More obstacles = more CBF constraints active simultaneously. When the robot
must thread through narrow gaps between multiple obstacles, phi's robustness
margin becomes critical. The policy should learn to increase phi when multiple
obstacles are nearby.

Setup:
- 6 obstacles (not 3), true radius=5.0 each
- Action space: [kx, ky, alpha, phi]
- ISSf-CBF: Lgh @ u >= -alpha * h(x) + (||Lgh||^2 * phi) / h(x)
- Radius noise: error ~ U(-1.0, +1.0) per obstacle per episode
- Constant bias: magnitude ~ U(0.0, 1.0), random direction per episode
- Randomized placement, min_spacing=11m (tighter than 3-obs experiments)
- Observation: 23 dims (6*3 + 3 + 2)
- Eval: error=-1.0, bias=0.7 upward
