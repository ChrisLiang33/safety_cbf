====================================================================================

Question:

does the ISSf-CBF show stronger phi adaptation? This is the most realistic scenario —
the robot has wrong obstacle size estimates AND gets pushed by persistent external forces.

Key changes from exp 26:
  - Added constant bias disturbance: robot_pos += safe_u * dt + bias * dt
  - Bias sampled ONCE per episode (magnitude ~ U(0.0, 1.0), random direction)
  - This avoids CLT averaging — bias is persistent throughout the episode

Setup:
- ISSf-CBF agent: [kx, ky, alpha, phi] action space
- Radius noise: error ~ U(-1.0, +1.0) per obstacle per episode
- Constant bias: magnitude ~ U(0.0, 1.0), random direction, per episode

- 2M PPO timesteps, 8 parallel envs

  - Dynamics: 0.7 * 0.1 * 300 = 21m cumulative drift over episode
  - Together: robot has wrong model AND gets pushed into obstacles
