Experiment 49: Fixed Alpha=4.0 + Combined Best
=================================================
NOT YET RUN

Based on: Exp 48 (fixed alpha + combined best) with alpha=4.0 instead of 1.0

Motivation:
-----------
Alpha=4.0 is very aggressive — the CBF constraint is very loose, allowing
the robot to approach obstacles at high speed. This makes phi absolutely
critical: without phi adding a robustness margin, the robot will collide
frequently. This should create the strongest possible gradient signal for
phi learning.

The risk is that alpha=4.0 may cause so many collisions during training
that the policy can't learn to navigate at all.

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, phi] — alpha FIXED at 4.0
- Max speed: 6 m/s, target at x=150, 800 max steps
- Sensor error: systematic bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0)
- Obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0)
- Collision penalty: -250
- 10M steps, 16 envs

Comparisons:
------------
- vs Exp 48 (alpha=1.0): does much higher alpha push phi to learn more?
- vs Exp 50 (alpha=2.0): three-way comparison at 1.0, 2.0, 4.0

Potential concerns:
-------------------
- Alpha=4.0 is at the upper bound of the alpha action space in
  experiments where alpha is learned. Very aggressive.
- May cause too many collisions, making training unstable.
- Navigation success rate could be very low.
