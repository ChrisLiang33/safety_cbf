Experiment 50: Fixed Alpha=2.0 + Combined Best
=================================================
NOT YET RUN

Based on: Exp 48 (fixed alpha + combined best) with alpha=2.0 instead of 1.0

Motivation:
-----------
Exp 48 uses alpha=1.0 fixed. Alpha=2.0 is more aggressive — it makes the
CBF constraint looser (-alpha*h is larger when alpha is bigger, allowing
more control authority near obstacles).

With a looser baseline constraint, the robot can approach obstacles faster.
This makes phi MORE critical — without phi adding a robustness margin, the
robot risks getting dangerously close. Phi has both more room and more
reason to act. This may produce the strongest phi learning signal.

However, alpha=2.0 also means more collisions during training, which could
help or hurt learning depending on whether the policy can extract the right
lesson from those failures.

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, phi] — alpha FIXED at 2.0
- Max speed: 6 m/s, target at x=150, 800 max steps
- Sensor error: systematic bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0)
- Obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0)
- Collision penalty: -250
- 10M steps, 16 envs

Comparisons:
------------
- vs Exp 48 (alpha=1.0): does higher alpha push phi to learn more?
- vs Exp 49 (alpha=0.5): three-way comparison of alpha's effect on phi

Potential concerns:
-------------------

  navigation quality. If success rate is very low (<3/10), the alpha
