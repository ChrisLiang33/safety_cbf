Motivation:
-----------
This is the KEY experiment for the SHIELD SDF formulation. Exp 50
(squared CBF + fixed alpha=2.0) showed our strongest phi learning
(phi 0.1-0.8, visible proximity structure). Does the SHIELD SDF
produce even better phi behavior?

The SHIELD SDF's phi term is conceptually aligned with Theorem 2's
projected covariance: ||Lgh||^2 already captures only the e_i
direction, so phi learns to approximate the uncertainty magnitude
along the obstacle direction.
