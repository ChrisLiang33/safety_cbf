Experiment 20: Tight Obstacles (ISSf-CBF Narrow Gap Test)
==========================================================

Question:
Exp 18 showed both agents handle noise=0.3 easily with wide obstacle spacing.
Does tightening the obstacle layout (forcing narrower gaps) make phi essential?

Hypothesis: with obstacles at y=±3.5-5 instead of ±5-8 and x gaps tightened
(25-30, 40-50, 55-65 instead of 20-35, 45-60, 65-80), the robot must navigate
through narrower corridors. The tighter spacing means:
  1. h(x) values are smaller (closer to obstacles) → stronger 1/h amplification
  2. Less room for error → noise has proportionally larger effect
  3. Alpha-only agent may not be conservative enough in tight gaps

Setup:
- Dynamic alpha+phi agent: [kx, ky, alpha, phi] action space (ISSf-CBF)
- Dynamic alpha-only agent: [kx, ky, alpha] action space (standard CBF)
- Both trained fresh with same noise and tight obstacle layout
- Obstacle positions (tighter than exp 18):
  - Obs 1: x in [25, 30], y in [3.5, 5.0]
  - Obs 2: x in [40, 50], y in [-5.0, -3.5]
  - Obs 3: x in [55, 65], y in [3.5, 5.0]
- Obstacle radius: 5.0 (same as exp 18)
- Training disturbance: [0.0, 0.5], Eval noise: 0.3 (same as exp 18)
- 100m map, single-integrator dynamics, 2M PPO timesteps, 8 parallel envs
