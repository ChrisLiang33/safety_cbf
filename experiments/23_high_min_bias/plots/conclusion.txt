Experiment 23: High Minimum Bias Training
==========================================

Question:
  Does training with higher minimum bias (0.5-1.0 vs 0.0-1.0) force the policy
  to learn stronger alpha/phi correlation with obstacle proximity?

Hypothesis:
  By always facing significant bias during training, the policy should learn that
  phi is essential near obstacles and show clearer alpha-decrease + phi-increase
  near obstacle zones.

Setup:
  - Same as Experiment 22 (constant bias, 3-obstacle weave, ISSf-CBF vs standard CBF)
  - Training disturbance_range changed from U(0.0, 1.0) to U(0.5, 1.0)
  - Eval bias magnitude kept at 0.7 for fair comparison with exp 22
  - 2M timesteps, PPO, 8 parallel envs
  - 8 hand-picked scenarios + 100 random scenarios
  - Both alpha+phi (ISSf-CBF) and alpha-only (standard CBF) retrained fresh
