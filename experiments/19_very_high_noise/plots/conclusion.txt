Experiment 19: Very High Noise Phi-CBF (ISSf-CBF Extreme Stress Test)
=====================================================================

Question:
Exp 18 showed both alpha+phi and alpha-only achieve 0% violations at noise=0.3.
Can we break the standard CBF by pushing noise even higher (0.7 eval, training up to 1.0)?

Hypothesis: at noise scale 0.7 (2.3x higher than exp 18), the standard CBF will finally
start accumulating safety violations because the disturbance magnitude approaches the
control authority. The ISSf-CBF's phi term should provide the formal robustness margin
needed to prevent these violations.

Setup:
- Dynamic alpha+phi agent: [kx, ky, alpha, phi] action space (ISSf-CBF)
  - kx/ky in [-3, 3], alpha in [0.1, 5.0], phi in [0.01, 10.0]
- Dynamic alpha-only agent: [kx, ky, alpha] action space (standard CBF)
  - kx/ky in [-3, 3], alpha in [0.1, 5.0]
  - Both agents trained fresh in this experiment with same noise
- ISSf-CBF RHS: -alpha * h(x) + (||Lgh||^2 * phi) / max(h(x), 1e-4)
- Standard CBF RHS: -alpha * h(x)
- Training disturbance: uniform noise, scale domain-randomized [0.0, 1.0] per episode
  (2x higher than exp 18's [0.0, 0.5], 10x higher than exp 15)
- Eval disturbance: noise scale = 0.7 (2.3x higher than exp 18's 0.3)
- Dynamics: robot_pos += safe_u * dt + noise * dt
- 3 obstacles (radius=5.0), same slalom layout as exps 9/13/14/15/18
- 100m map, single-integrator dynamics, 2M PPO timesteps, 8 parallel envs
