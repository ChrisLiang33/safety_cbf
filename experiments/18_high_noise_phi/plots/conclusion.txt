Experiment 18: High-Noise Phi-CBF (ISSf-CBF Stress Test)
=========================================================

Question:
Does phi become essential under high disturbance? Experiments 14-15 showed phi stays
low because noise was too small (scale 0.05) — the standard CBF handled it fine with
0% collisions, making phi a "comfort margin" rather than a necessity. Here we crank
noise to 5-6x higher levels to stress-test the ISSf-CBF framework.

Hypothesis: at noise scale 0.3, the standard CBF (no phi) will start accumulating
safety violations because the disturbance can push the robot through the CBF barrier
between timesteps. The ISSf-CBF's phi term (||Lgh||^2 * phi / h(x)) should provide
the formal robustness margin needed to prevent these violations. We expect to see:
  1. Alpha-only agent (standard CBF): non-zero collision rate at high noise
  2. Alpha+phi agent (ISSf-CBF): 0% or near-0% collisions
  3. Phi visibly increases near obstacles — the agent learned phi is necessary
  4. Alpha decreases near obstacles while phi increases — the expected trade-off

Setup:
- Dynamic alpha+phi agent: [kx, ky, alpha, phi] action space (ISSf-CBF)
  - kx/ky in [-3, 3], alpha in [0.1, 5.0], phi in [0.01, 10.0]
- Dynamic alpha-only agent: [kx, ky, alpha] action space (standard CBF)
  - kx/ky in [-3, 3], alpha in [0.1, 5.0]
  - Both agents trained fresh in this experiment with same noise
- ISSf-CBF RHS: -alpha * h(x) + (||Lgh||^2 * phi) / max(h(x), 1e-4)
- Standard CBF RHS: -alpha * h(x)
- Training disturbance: uniform noise, scale domain-randomized [0.0, 0.5] per episode
  (5x higher than exp 15's [0.0, 0.1])
- Eval disturbance: noise scale = 0.3 (6x higher than exp 15's 0.05)
- Dynamics: robot_pos += safe_u * dt + noise * dt
- 3 obstacles (radius=5.0), same slalom layout as exps 9/13/14/15
- 100m map, single-integrator dynamics, 2M PPO timesteps, 8 parallel envs
