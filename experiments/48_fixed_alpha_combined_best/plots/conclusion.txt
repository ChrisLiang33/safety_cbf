Experiment 48: Fixed Alpha=1.0 + Combined Best
============================================

Based on: Exp 43 (fixed alpha) + Exp 46 (combined best)

Motivation:
-----------
Combine everything: fixed alpha forces phi to be the only knob, higher
speed gives room for speed contrast, coherent sensor error gives clean
learning signal, collision -250 adds pressure.

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, phi] — alpha FIXED at 1.0
- Max speed: 6 m/s, target at x=150, 800 max steps
- Sensor error: systematic bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0), obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0)
- Collision penalty: -250
- 10M steps, 16 envs

Results:
--------
- ~9/10 reached — excellent navigation
- Speed: near max (~6 m/s) throughout, no dramatic dips
- Phi: more activity than exp 47. Range ~0.01-0.5, some scenarios show
  phi increasing at closer distances. Collision penalty IS helping.
  Still not as clean as exp 43's phi behavior.
- Speed vs distance aggregate: clear speed drop at close distances

Conclusion:
-----------
Collision penalty (-250) adds value on top of fixed alpha — exp 48 shows
more phi activity than exp 47 (-100). But the "avoid rather than engage"
pattern persists: the environment is wide enough that the robot steers
around obstacles rather than passing close, so phi is rarely stressed.

Key problem: the map is wide enough that going around is always faster
than going through. The robot has no reason to approach closely.

Potential fix: narrow the corridor (tighter y-range or more obstacles)
to force unavoidable close encounters. The robot MUST pass near obstacles
regardless of steering, creating the conditions where phi is truly needed.
