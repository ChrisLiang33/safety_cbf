Experiment 52: Smooth SDF-Based CBF
=====================================
NOT YET RUN

Based on: Exp 45 setup with smoothed SDF from paper (equation 20)

Motivation:
-----------
Following the paper's formulation, use a smoothed signed distance function:
  h(x) = lambda * (1 - e^{-gamma * sdf(x)})
  where sdf(x) = ||x - obs|| - r

Properties:
  - Saturates at lambda far from obstacles (h -> lambda)
  - Goes to 0 at obstacle surface (h -> 0)
  - Gradient is strong near obstacles, weak far away
  - dh/dx = lambda * gamma * e^{-gamma*sdf} * unit_vector
  - This naturally focuses the CBF constraint where it matters

Parameters: lambda=1.0, gamma=0.5

Compared to exp 51 (simple SDF), the smooth version:
  - Reduces chattering when switching closest obstacle
  - Creates naturally proximity-weighted constraint strength
  - May help phi learning by making the constraint more "felt" near obstacles

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, alpha, phi]
- CBF: h = lambda*(1 - e^{-gamma*sdf}), lambda=1.0, gamma=0.5
- Max speed: 6 m/s, target at x=150, 800 max steps
- Sensor error: systematic bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0), obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0)
- Collision penalty: -100, progress*50
- 10M steps, 16 envs

Comparisons:
------------
- vs Exp 45: squared CBF (original)
- vs Exp 51: simple SDF CBF
- Three-way comparison of CBF formulations on same environment

Potential concerns:
-------------------
- Lambda and gamma values may need tuning. gamma=0.5 means the
  constraint starts engaging around sdf~2m (e^{-1} ~ 0.37).
  If too conservative or too loose, adjust gamma.
