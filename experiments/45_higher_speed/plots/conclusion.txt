Experiment 45: Higher Speed + Systematic Sensor Error
======================================================
NOT YET RUN

Based on: Experiment 44 (systematic sensor error model)

Motivation:
-----------
In exp 40, alpha showed some proximity-adaptive behavior (dropping near obstacles),
but the effect was hard to see because the max speed of 3 m/s was too low. The CBF
constraint with alpha=2 vs alpha=0.5 both allow speeds near 3 m/s — no visible
speed contrast. By raising the speed ceiling, we expect to see a clear difference:
  - Open area: robot goes fast (5-6 m/s), alpha high
  - Near obstacle: alpha drops, CBF actively constrains speed down to 2-3 m/s
  - Speed plot shows visible dip near obstacles that correlates with alpha

Modifications from Exp 44:
--------------------------
1. Max speed: 3 m/s -> 6 m/s (action space [-6, 6])
2. Environment: target at x=150 (was 100), wider obstacle bands
3. Obstacle y-range: [-10, 10] (was [-8, 8])
4. OOB bounds: x [-5, 160], y [-20, 20]
5. Max steps: 800 (was 600)
6. Speed plot ylim: 8 (was 4.5)
7. Plot xlim: [-5, 165]

Kept from Exp 44:
-----------------
- Systematic sensor bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
- Dynamics bias U(0.3, 1.0)
- Obstacle radius U(3.0, 7.0)
- Action: [kx, ky, alpha, phi]
- 10M training steps, 16 envs
- Collision penalty: -100 (unchanged)
- Progress reward: progress * 50.0 - 1.0 (unchanged)

What we hope to find:
---------------------
1. Alpha: clear up-down pattern — high in open, low near obstacles, visible
   in both the alpha time series and the speed plot
2. Phi: with higher speeds + sensor uncertainty, there's more danger near
   obstacles, which may create stronger gradient signal for phi learning
3. Speed: visible contrast between open-area cruising and obstacle approach

Potential concerns / fixes if results are off:
----------------------------------------------
- Collision penalty may be too weak at 6 m/s. At 3 m/s, reward per step is
  ~15 (0.3m * 50), so collision -100 = ~7 steps of reward. At 6 m/s, reward
  per step is ~30 (0.6m * 50), so collision -100 = ~3 steps of reward.
  The relative cost of colliding is halved. If the robot is too reckless,
  try scaling collision penalty to -200 to maintain the same ratio.
  However, CBF should enforce safety regardless of reward, so this may not
  matter. If the robot IS reckless, that could actually help phi learning
  by creating more pressure.
