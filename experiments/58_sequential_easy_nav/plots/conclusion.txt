Experiment 58: Easy Nav (Frozen) → Hard Safety Filter
======================================================
NOT YET RUN

Based on: Exp 56 with key improvement — Phase 1 trains with NO obstacles

Motivation:
-----------
Exp 56 trained nav on the same map as safety, so the frozen nav policy
still had some obstacle avoidance behavior. This experiment trains Phase 1
with NO obstacles at all — the nav policy learns ONLY to beeline toward
the target. When frozen and placed on a hard map, it will drive straight
at obstacles, giving alpha/phi maximum training signal.

Phase 2 also uses tighter obstacle placement (y: [-6,6] instead of [-10,10])
so obstacles are closer to the direct path.

Architecture:
-------------
Phase 1: NavEasyEnv
  - Action: [kx, ky]
  - NO obstacles — obstacles placed at (500, 500) effectively invisible
  - 5M steps (less needed for simple task)

Phase 2: SafetyHardEnv
  - Action: [alpha, phi]
  - kx, ky from frozen Phase 1 model (beelines to target)
  - SHIELD SDF CBF, collision -250
  - Obstacles at y=[-6, 6] — closer to direct path
  - 10M steps

Comparisons:
------------
- vs Exp 56: same architecture but nav trained WITH obstacles
  Exp 58 should show more CBF activation and stronger alpha/phi signal
- vs Exp 57: A* nav vs dumb-beeline nav on hard map
  Different navigation strategies, same safety training setup

What we hope to find:
---------------------
1. CBF activates much more frequently than exp 56
2. Stronger, cleaner alpha/phi proximity-adaptive behavior
3. The frozen beeline nav creates unavoidable close encounters
