Experiment 62: Learned Nav + Moving Obstacles (Two-Policy)
===========================================================
NOT YET RUN

Motivation:
-----------
Two-policy sequential architecture with moving obstacles.
Phase 1: Learn to navigate with moving obstacles (kx, ky only, no CBF)
Phase 2: Freeze nav, train safety filter (alpha, phi) with SHIELD SDF

Moving obstacles add a new dimension of uncertainty that static
environments don't test. The frozen nav policy will sometimes drive
toward obstacles that have moved into its path, forcing the CBF
to intervene.

Architecture:
-------------
Phase 1: NavMovingObsEnv
  - Action: [kx, ky], no CBF
  - Moving obstacles (0.3-1.0 m/s, bounce off boundaries)
  - No sensor error (clean perception for nav training)
  - 10M steps

Nav Success Check: 500 episodes, 70% threshold

Phase 2: SafetyMovingObsEnv
  - Action: [alpha, phi]
  - Frozen nav policy from Phase 1
  - SHIELD SDF CBF, collision -250
  - Sensor error: systematic bias + jitter
  - 10M steps

Comparisons:
------------
- vs Exp 61: A* vs learned nav with moving obstacles
- vs Exp 56/58: static vs moving obstacles with two-policy architecture
