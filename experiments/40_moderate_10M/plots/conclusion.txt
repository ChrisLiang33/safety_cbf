Experiment 40: Moderate Difficulty + 10M Steps
================================================

Motivation:
-----------
Earlier experiments (34-36) used 5M steps and showed phi stuck at 0.01.
Does doubling training time to 10M under moderate conditions let the
policy learn phi?

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, alpha, phi] — full ISSf-CBF
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0), random angle, constant per episode
- Radius error: U(-1.0, 0.0), independent per obstacle (always underestimates)
- Obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0), random per obstacle
- Training: 10M steps, 16 envs, standard reward (progress*50, collision -100)

What we hoped to find:
----------------------
Alpha decreasing near obstacles (proximity-adaptive) and phi increasing
near obstacles (robustness margin against sensor error and dynamics bias).

What we found:
--------------
Original plots (fixed eval: bias=0.7, error=-1.0, radius=5.0):
  - 8/10 reached — solid navigation improvement over exp 34 (4/8)
  - Alpha: some proximity-related movement but not strongly visible due
    to max speed of 3 m/s being too low to show speed contrast
  - Phi: mostly flat near 0.01, slight movement to ~0.22 in some scenarios
    but no consistent proximity-adaptive trend

Randomized plots (per-obstacle radius/error, per-scenario bias):
  - 8/10 reached — consistent with fixed eval
  - Alpha: similar pattern across varied conditions
  - Phi: still mostly flat at 0.01 in aggregate policy map

Conclusion:
-----------
More training improved navigation success but phi remains largely unused.
Alpha + kx/ky are sufficient to handle moderate disturbances, giving the
policy no gradient pressure to learn phi. The radius error being independent
per obstacle (averaging effect) may also dilute the learning signal.
The speed ceiling of 3 m/s may also mask alpha's adaptive behavior since
CBF constraints at different alpha values all allow near-max speed.
