Experiment 33: Level 3 — CBF with Alpha + Constant Bias Disturbance
=====================================================================

Question:
The standard CBF assumes x_dot = u, but reality is x_dot = u + bias.
Does this model mismatch cause the CBF to fail (collisions)?
This establishes the NEED for phi (Level 4).

Setup:
- Action: [kx, ky, alpha] — standard CBF with QP safety filter
- CBF: Lgh @ u >= -alpha * h(x)
- Constant bias: magnitude ~ U(0.0, 1.0), random direction, per episode
- Eval: bias magnitude = 0.7, upward direction for hand-picked
- No radius noise
- Gentler x-band randomization, 5M timesteps, 16 envs

Expected:
- Some collisions under strong bias (CBF can't account for unmodeled force)
- Alpha may increase near obstacles to compensate, but has limits
- Performance degradation compared to Level 2

Observations:
- Hand-picked scenarios (bias=0.7, direction=upward):
  1. Straight Path — FAILED
  2. Obstacles on Path — FAILED
  3. All Upper — REACHED
  4. All Lower — REACHED
  5. Narrow Gap — REACHED
  6. Clustered — REACHED
  7. Spread — FAILED
  8. Off-Center Target — REACHED
  3/8 scenarios fail. Failures occur when obstacles are in the robot's path AND
  the upward bias pushes toward them.

- Alpha adaptation (col 2):
  Alpha still shows some reactivity to obstacles but the pattern is noisier than
  Level 2. The bias disrupts the policy's ability to cleanly adapt alpha.

- Aggregate policy map (alpha vs distance):
  Correlation is weaker and messier than Level 2. Alpha range compressed (~0.2-1.75
  vs 0.1-2.1 in Level 2). Some scenarios show erratic alpha at large distances.
  The clean "high alpha far, low alpha near" pattern from Level 2 is degraded.

- Comparison to Level 2:
  Level 2: all 8 scenarios reached, clean alpha-distance correlation
  Level 3: 3/8 fail, noisy alpha adaptation under bias

Conclusion:
The standard CBF constraint (Lgh @ u >= -alpha * h(x)) assumes x_dot = u, but
reality is x_dot = u + bias. Alpha controls how aggressively the CBF reacts to
the current state, but it has NO term to account for the persistent unmodeled force.
No matter how the policy tunes alpha, the constraint is fundamentally missing the
bias — it cannot formally guarantee safety under disturbance.

This directly motivates the ISSf-CBF (Level 4), which adds the phi term:
  Lgh @ u >= -alpha * h(x) + (||Lgh||^2 * phi) / h(x)
The phi/h(x) term grows near obstacles, creating extra margin to absorb the
effect of the disturbance that alpha alone cannot handle.
