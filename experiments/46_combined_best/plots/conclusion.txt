Experiment 46: Combined Best
=============================
NOT YET RUN

Based on: Exp 45 (higher speed + systematic sensor error) + Exp 42 (collision penalty)

Motivation:
-----------
Combining the best components from previous experiments:
  - Exp 42 showed that higher collision penalty creates more phi activity
    (phi moved from flat 0.01 up to ~1.0, though noisy)
  - Exp 44/45 introduced systematic sensor error (coherent signal) and
    higher speed (to make alpha behavior visible in speed plots)
  - This experiment combines all three: higher speed gives alpha room to
    show contrast, systematic sensor error gives phi a coherent learning
    signal, and moderate collision penalty creates pressure to use phi.

Components:
-----------
From Exp 44: Systematic sensor bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
From Exp 45: Max speed 6 m/s, target at x=150, 800 max steps
From Exp 42: Collision penalty -250 (moderate between -100 and -500)

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, alpha, phi]
- Max speed: 6 m/s (action space [-6, 6])
- Environment: target at x=150, obstacle bands [20-50, 60-100, 110-140]
- Collision penalty: -250
- Progress reward: progress * 50.0 - 1.0
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0)
- Obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0)
- 10M steps, 16 envs

What we hope to find:
---------------------
1. Alpha: clear speed contrast — fast in open areas, slow near obstacles
2. Phi: adaptive proximity behavior driven by both the coherent sensor
   error signal AND the higher collision penalty pressure
3. Better phi learning than any individual experiment alone

Potential concerns / fixes:
---------------------------
- If collision penalty -250 is still too aggressive, try -200
- If navigation suffers (low success rate), the combined difficulty of
  higher speed + uncertainty + penalty may be too much — consider
  training for 15-20M steps instead
