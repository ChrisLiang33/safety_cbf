Experiment 46: Combined Best
=============================

Based on: Exp 45 (higher speed) + Exp 42 (collision penalty)

Motivation:
-----------
Combine the best components from previous experiments:
  - From Exp 44: systematic sensor bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
  - From Exp 45: max speed 6 m/s, target at x=150, 800 max steps
  - From Exp 42: collision penalty -250

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, alpha, phi]
- Max speed: 6 m/s, target at x=150
- Sensor error: systematic bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0), obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0)
- Collision penalty: -250, progress*50
- 10M steps, 16 envs

Results:
--------
- ~8-9/10 reached — strong navigation
- Speed: pegged at ~6 m/s throughout, no visible drops near obstacles.
  Same behavior as exp 45 — robot steers around at distance rather than
  slowing down.
- Alpha: clear proximity-adaptive behavior, drops near obstacles. Visible
  spikes in alpha time series correlating with obstacle zones.
- Phi: slightly more activity than exp 45 (range ~0.05-0.35). The higher
  collision penalty pushed phi up a bit, but still no clean proximity-
  adaptive trend. Alpha remains the dominant safety knob.

Conclusion:
-----------
The collision penalty nudged phi slightly but didn't fundamentally change
the picture. With alpha still available, the policy prefers alpha + steering
over phi. Same conclusion as exp 45 — the robot avoids obstacles at
distance and goes max speed, so phi is never stressed.

Reinforces that fixed alpha (exp 47-50) is likely the key ingredient.

Future improvements to eval:
----------------------------
- Remove aggregate policy map (not very informative)
- Add a success rate metric that runs ~1000 randomized episodes to get
  a statistically meaningful success rate, since we're already training
  with full domain randomization
