Experiment 41: Phi Min Rebalance
=================================

Motivation:
-----------
Phi was stuck at 0.01 in earlier experiments. Two changes were made:
1. Phi floor raised from 0.01 to 0.5 — force the policy to always use some phi
2. Progress reward halved: 50 -> 25 — reduce penalty for slowing down

Setup:
------
- Action: [kx, ky, alpha, phi], phi range [0.5, 10.0] (was [0.01, 10.0])
- Progress reward: progress * 25.0 (was * 50.0)
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0), radius error: U(-1.0, 0.0)
- Obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0), 10M steps, 16 envs

What we hoped to find:
----------------------
That raising the phi floor + reducing progress pressure would let phi
learn adaptive proximity behavior (going above 0.5 near obstacles).

Results:
--------
- 7/10 reached (slightly worse than exp 40's 8/10)
- Alpha: narrower range (~1.0-1.75), less dynamic than exp 40
- Phi: pinned at 0.5 (the floor) for nearly everything. One scenario
  showed a brief spike to ~0.7 but no consistent trend.
- Speed: generally lower due to halved progress reward

Conclusion:
-----------
This experiment was not very meaningful. Artificially inflating the phi
floor just shifts the flat line from 0.01 to 0.5 — the policy still
treats phi as a constant it doesn't vary. The reduced progress reward
hurt navigation without helping phi learn. The problem isn't that phi
starts too low; it's that the policy has no gradient signal telling it
"higher phi = safer near obstacles." Raising the floor doesn't create
that signal.
