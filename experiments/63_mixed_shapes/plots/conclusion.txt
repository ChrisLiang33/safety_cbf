Experiment 63: Stochastic A* + Mixed Obstacle Shapes
======================================================
NOT YET RUN

Motivation:
-----------
All previous experiments used circular obstacles only. Real environments
have walls, boxes, irregular shapes. This experiment tests whether the
general SDF formulation works with mixed shapes.

Also, A* is now stochastic — waypoints are perturbed by random noise
(+/- 2m) to give alpha/phi more varied training signal.

Obstacle shapes:
----------------
- Circle: sdf = ||p - center|| - radius
- Rectangle: sdf = distance to nearest edge of axis-aligned box
- Line segment (wall): sdf = distance to nearest point on segment - thickness
- 4 obstacles per episode, shape randomly chosen per obstacle

A* stochastic:
--------------
- A* computes optimal path, then each waypoint is perturbed by U(-2, 2)m
- Same path won't repeat even in identical layouts
- Forces policy to handle varied approach angles near obstacles

Setup:
------
- Action: [alpha, phi] — kx, ky from stochastic A*
- SHIELD SDF CBF, collision -250
- 4 mixed-shape obstacles
- Sensor error: systematic bias + jitter
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0)
- 10M steps, 16 envs
