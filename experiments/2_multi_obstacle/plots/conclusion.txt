Experiment 2: Multi Obstacle (2 Obstacles)
==========================================

Question:
Does dynamic alpha scale from 1 obstacle to 2? With two CBF constraints active
simultaneously, the QP feasible region shrinks. Can the RL agent still learn a
useful alpha adaptation strategy when navigating between two obstacles?

Setup:
- Dynamic agent: [alpha, kx, ky] action space (alpha in [0.1, 5.0], kx/ky in [-2, 2])
- Baselines: fixed alpha = [0.1, 0.5, 1.0, 5.0] with learned [kx, ky] steering
- 2 obstacles (radius=1.0 each): obs1 at x in [2,5], obs2 at x in [5.5,8.5]
- Target at x=10, randomized y in [-4,4], radius in [0.5,2.0]
- Single-integrator dynamics, 900K PPO timesteps, 8 parallel envs
- Reward: progress (50x), time penalty (-0.5), lateral penalty (-0.1*|ky|)

Observations:
- Trajectories (9 hand-picked scenarios):
  - Dynamic alpha navigates around both obstacles successfully in all scenarios.
  - Alpha color along trajectories shows two distinct adaptation events — one per
    obstacle — with alpha dropping as the robot approaches each obstacle and
    recovering in between.
  - Fixed alpha baselines all reach the target in most scenarios. The trajectories
    diverge more when obstacles are tightly spaced (e.g., "Tight Corridor").

- Alpha Adaptation:
  - Two clear alpha dips are visible in the time series, one for each obstacle.
  - The dip depth and duration scale with how close the robot passes to each obstacle.
  - Between obstacles, alpha recovers toward higher values, showing the agent
    learned per-obstacle modulation rather than a blanket conservative strategy.

- Aggregate Metrics (100 random scenarios):
  - Target Reached: alpha=0.1 68%, alpha=0.5 99%, alpha=1.0/5.0 100%, Dynamic 100%.
  - Safety Violations: 0% across all methods.
  - Safety Margin: alpha=0.1 ~2.49m, alpha=0.5 ~1.68m, Dynamic ~0.90m,
    alpha=5.0 ~0.68m. Dynamic sits between alpha=1.0 and alpha=5.0.
  - Path Efficiency: alpha=0.1 ~1.24 (large detours), Dynamic ~0.97, near-optimal.

Conclusion:
Dynamic alpha scales cleanly from 1 to 2 obstacles. The agent learns per-obstacle
alpha modulation — lowering alpha independently as each obstacle is encountered,
then recovering speed in between. 100% success rate with 0% collisions and near-
optimal path efficiency. The gap between dynamic and fixed alpha widens with 2
obstacles: alpha=0.1 drops to 68% success (from 76% in exp 1), while dynamic
maintains 100%.

The safety margin (~0.90m) is tighter than exp 1 (~1.27m), reflecting the more
constrained environment. The agent trades margin for efficiency, which is the
correct tradeoff when the CBF guarantees safety regardless.
