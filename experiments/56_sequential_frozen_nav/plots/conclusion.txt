Experiment 56: Sequential — Frozen Nav → Train Alpha/Phi
=========================================================
NOT YET RUN

Motivation:
-----------
Joint training of [kx, ky, alpha, phi] fails because kx/ky dominate
the gradient signal. The robot learns to navigate so well that the CBF
never activates, starving alpha/phi of signal.

Solution: train sequentially.
  Phase 1: Train kx/ky only (no CBF). Robot learns to navigate.
  Phase 2: FREEZE kx/ky. Train alpha/phi only with SHIELD SDF CBF.

With navigation frozen, the robot WILL be brought near obstacles by
the nav layer. It cannot learn to avoid — that behavior is locked in.
Alpha/phi are forced to activate and receive consistent gradient signal.

Architecture:
-------------
Phase 1: NavOnlyEnv
  - Action: [kx, ky]
  - No CBF, no QP
  - Same env geometry (6 m/s, target x=150)
  - 10M steps

Phase 2: SafetyFilterEnv
  - Action: [alpha, phi]
  - kx, ky from frozen Phase 1 model (model.predict, deterministic)
  - SHIELD SDF CBF (Eq 19-22), single constraint
  - Collision -250
  - 10M steps

Setup:
------
- SHIELD SDF: lambda=1.0, gamma=0.5
- Sensor error: systematic bias U(-0.6, 0.4) + jitter U(-0.2, 0.2)
- Dynamics bias: U(0.3, 1.0), obstacle radius: U(3.0, 7.0)
- Max speed: 6 m/s, target at x=150, 800 max steps

What we hope to find:
---------------------
1. Alpha/phi get consistent training signal (CBF activates frequently)
2. Alpha adapts: high in open space, low near obstacles
3. Phi shows proximity-adaptive behavior
4. Better alpha/phi learning than any joint-training experiment

Note: FPS may be lower because Phase 2 calls model.predict() on the
frozen nav policy every step (extra inference overhead per env step).
